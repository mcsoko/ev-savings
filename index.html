How this is calculated
Gas cost Distance ÷ Palisade MPG × gas price.
Tesla driving consumption Distance × Tesla Wh/mi.
Charging losses Tesla trip-computer efficiency represents energy used by the car. The calculator estimates additional electricity drawn from the wall to account for charging losses.
Charging mix Home and Supercharger electricity prices are weighted according to the selected charging percentages.
What isn't included Insurance, depreciation, financing, registration and fixed ownership costs are intentionally excluded. This calculator answers: "What did it cost to drive these miles?"
